Who We Are

Established to Address Human Capital Challenges

The Council for Humanitarian Empowerment & Natural Disasters (CHEND) is an organisation built to tackle human capital and knowledge economy challenges in our communities.

CHEND provides structured training programmes and consultancies for Small & Medium-Sized Enterprises as well as corporate institutions and companies. During the course of delivering its core mandate, the Council identified a critical felt need for financial and knowledge capital — leading to the establishment of Kick Poverty Microfinance as a subsidiary.

Mission

Empowering People Through Knowledge & Capital

To empower individuals, enterprises, and communities through targeted training, consultancy, and financial access — leaving no one behind.

Vision

A Self-Reliant & Thriving Society

A society where every individual regardless of circumstance has access to the resources and knowledge required to contribute to economic and social development.

What We Do

Our Core Programmes

Structured interventions designed to build capacity, promote enterprise, and strengthen community resilience.

Structured Training Programmes

Competency-based training for individuals and enterprises across vocational, entrepreneurial, and professional development tracks, tailored for SMEs and corporate institutions.

Corporate Consultancy

Expert advisory services helping small businesses, NGOs, and corporate organisations solve strategic challenges and strengthen operations through evidence-based recommendations.

Microfinance & Financial Access

Through our subsidiary Kick Poverty Microfinance, we provide soft loans to women in business, youths needing college fees, and property owners — making financial capital accessible to those most in need.

Who We Serve

Our Target Beneficiaries

CHEND's work is centred on the most vulnerable members of our society. Every programme, consultancy, and financial service is designed with their needs at the forefront.

We believe that meaningful empowerment starts by meeting people where they are — providing resources, skills, and financial tools that create lasting change at the individual, household, and community levels.

Orphans — children without parental support who need educational and welfare assistance.

Widows — women who have lost their spouses and require economic and psychosocial support.

People Living with Disability — individuals requiring inclusive support and livelihood opportunities.

The Aged (75 years & above) — elderly persons in need of welfare and dignity-centred care.

School-Going Children from Deprived Financial Backgrounds — learners whose education is threatened by poverty.

Our Subsidiary

Kick Poverty Microfinance

Born from the Council's discovery of the need for financial capital, Kick Po provides soft loans to women in business, youths for college fees, and property owners.
