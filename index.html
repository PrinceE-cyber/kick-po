Who We Are

A Council Built for Human Dignity

The Council for Humanitarian Empowerment & Natural Disasters (CHEND) was established to address human capital and knowledge economy challenges facing vulnerable communities in Sierra Leone.

During its core mandate, CHEND discovered the urgent need for financial and knowledge capital among the communities it served. This led to the birth of Kick Poverty Microfinance — a subsidiary that provides accessible soft loans to women in business, youth seeking college fees, and property owners.

Owned and led by Dr. Moses Simon (PhD, DBA), the company has been fully registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission and national statutory financial regulatory authorities.

The Story Behind It

The Birth of Kick Poverty

While fulfilling its core humanitarian mandate, CHEND encountered a recurring theme: communities lacked not just support — they lacked access to financial and knowledge capital to sustain themselves.

Following holistic research, the Council established Kick Poverty Microfinance as a subsidiary. The scheme was built to provide soft loans to women in business, youths seeking college fees, and property owners who need structured financial assistance.

The concept was initiated by Mariama Farmah, supported and sponsored by Dr. Moses Simon (PhD, DBA), who solely owns and leads the company today.
