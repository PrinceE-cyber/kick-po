How It Works

The Loan Process

1

Initial Enquiry

Contact our Credit Officers or Director of Credits & Administration to discuss your needs and eligibility.

2

Application & Screening

Submit your application. Our team assesses your capacity to repay, reviews your guarantor, and verifies collateral where required.

3

Approval

Successful applicants receive confirmation. Terms are communicated clearly and transparently before any agreement is signed.

4

Disbursement

Approved funds are disbursed promptly — especially for emergency cases where speed is critical.

5

Repayment

Repay on the agreed schedule. We work with clients to ensure repayment is manageable and timely.
