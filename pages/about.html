Our Story

How Kick Po Began

Kick Poverty Microfinance Company started in January 2024 with a skeletal staff and minimal resources. The idea was simple yet powerful: facilitate quicker access to small loans for clients in businesses and service-oriented workplaces — especially in emergency situations where time is critical.

The concept was initiated by Mariama Farmah and supported and sponsored by Dr. Moses Simon, who remains the sole owner of the company.

From day one, the company has been growing steadily — building trust, improving processes, and expanding its reach across the Western Area Rural District of Sierra Leone.
