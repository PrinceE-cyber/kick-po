Preamble & Background

The Council for Humanitarian Empowerment

The Council for Humanitarian Empowerment & Natural Disasters (CHEND) is an organisation established to address human capital and knowledge economy challenges facing communities across Sierra Leone.

The Council provides structured training programs and consultancies for Small & Medium-Sized Enterprises (SMEs), as well as corporate institutions and companies — equipping people with the knowledge and skills to thrive in a changing economy.

During the dispensation of its core mandate, CHEND discovered a felt need for financial and knowledge capital in the communities it serves. Following holistic research, the Council established Kick Poverty Microfinance as a subsidiary — providing soft loans to those who need it most.

Our Purpose

Mission & Values

🎯

Our Mission

To foster financial accessibility to women who are into business but are widowed or orphaned by natural or other circumstances — giving them the capital to stand on their own.

🌟

Our Vision

A Sierra Leone where every individual — regardless of background, disability, or circumstance — has access to the financial resources, training, and support needed to build a better life.

🤝

Our Approach

Holistic research-driven programs that combine structured training, professional consultancy, and accessible soft loans — screening applicants carefully to ensure sustainable outcomes for all parties.
