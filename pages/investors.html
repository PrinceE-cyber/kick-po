Investment Opportunities

Invest in Impact.
Earn Strong Returns.

Kick Poverty Microfinance welcomes investors seeking financially rewarding partnerships that also make a meaningful difference in Sierra Leone's communities.

Why Invest With Us

A Compelling Opportunity

Kick Poverty Microfinance is actively seeking investors. As at present, the company has no shareholders yet — meaning early investors will enter at ground level with the best terms and highest potential for growth.

📈

20% Annual Return

Investors are entitled to 20% of the total financial investment on an annual basis, cascaded after a minimum period of three months.

🔒

Capital Guaranteed

The actual amount invested is refunded in full on or at the stipulated date and time, as formalised in an official signed agreement.

📄

Official Agreement

An official agreement is signed and enforced for each and every investment — providing full legal protection and transparency for all parties.

Financial Terms

Investment Tiers

All investment amounts are governed by a signed official agreement. Returns are disbursed after a minimum period of three months.

Getting Started

How to Invest

Becoming an investor with Kick Poverty Microfinance is a straightforward process built on transparency and mutual agreement.

1

Express Interest

Contact Dr. Moses Simon or Ms. Mariama Farmah to express your interest in investing.

2

Discuss Terms

Our team will walk you through the investment tiers, return structure, and shareholder pathway.

3

Sign Agreement

An official investment agreement is drafted, reviewed, and signed by both parties for legal protection.

4

Earn Returns

Your 20% annual return is disbursed after a minimum of three months, as stipulated in the agreement.
