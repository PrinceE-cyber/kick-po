About the Scheme

What is Kick Poverty Microfinance?

Kick Poverty (Kick Po) Microfinance Company was established in January 2024 to facilitate quicker access to small loans for clients in businesses and service-oriented workplaces — especially in emergency situations.

The company disburses loans to qualified applicants who meet key assessment targets. Applicants are screened carefully to confirm their capacity to repay loans without delay. Guarantors play a crucial role in recommending applicants, and collateral may be required for exceptional or large loan amounts.

The company is fully registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission and all national statutory financial regulatory authorities, ensuring full compliance and accountability.

Our Products

Three Loan Categories

Each loan product is designed to address a specific financial need in our communities. All loans follow a structured assessment and disbursement process.

👩‍💼

Women in Business Loan

Business Loan

Designed specifically to empower female entrepreneurs — with a strong focus on widowed and single mothers who run their own businesses. This loan provides working capital for stock, equipment, market stalls, and other business needs.

The mission of Kick Poverty Microfinance is anchored in this product: fostering financial accessibility to women who are widowed or orphaned by natural or other circumstances, giving them the capital they need to provide for themselves and their families.

Primary Beneficiaries

Widows & Single Mothers in Business

Interest Rate

30% per annum

🎓

Youth Education Loan

Education Loan

Access to education should never be blocked by finances. Kick Poverty's education loan helps young people pay their college and tertiary institution fees, enabling them to focus on their studies and build their futures.

Applicants are assessed based on their enrolment status and their capacity — or that of a guarantor — to repay the loan upon graduation or after a defined period.

Primary Beneficiaries

Youths & College Students

Interest Rate

30% per annum

🏠

Property Loan

Property Loan

For property owners who require financial assistance for construction, renovation, or development. This loan is available to qualified owners who can provide collateral and meet the required assessment criteria.

Collateral is a standard requirement for this product given the larger loan amounts typically involved. An official signed agreement governs all property loan disbursements.

Primary Beneficiaries

Property Owners

Collateral

Required for exceptional amounts

Interest Rate

30% per annum

How It Works

The Application Process

We've designed a clear, transparent process to ensure applicants are properly assessed and loans are disbursed responsibly.

1

Enquire

Contact us via phone or WhatsApp to learn about available loan products and eligibility.

2

Apply

Submit your application with required documents, guarantor details, and collateral (if applicable).

3

Assessment

Our credit officers review your capacity to repay and verify your guarantor's recommendation.

4

Disbursement

Upon approval, your loan is disbursed and an official agreement is signed to formalise terms.
