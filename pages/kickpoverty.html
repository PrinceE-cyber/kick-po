About Kick Po

Bridging the Financial Gap for Those Who Need It Most

Kick Poverty (Kick Po) Microfinance Company started in January 2024 with a skeletal staff and minimal resources — but a clear, powerful mission.

The idea was born from a simple observation: too many hardworking people in our communities — especially women entrepreneurs and young people — are excluded from traditional financial services. Kick Po was established to change that, facilitating quicker access to small loans in business settings and emergency situations.

The concept was initiated by Mariama Farmah and supported and sponsored by Dr. Moses Simon. The company is solely owned by Moses Simon (PhD, DBA).

Fully Registered

Kick Po Microfinance is fully registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission and other national statutory financial regulatory authorities in Sierra Leone.

Our Mission

Foster Financial Accessibility to Women

Our mission is to foster financial accessibility to women who are into business but are widowed or orphaned by natural or other circumstances.

What We Offer

Our Loan Products

Kick Po offers three focused loan products designed for the real financial needs of our community members.

Women in Business

Soft loans tailored for women entrepreneurs — particularly widows and single mothers — to start, sustain, or grow their businesses. Fast processing with minimal bureaucracy.

Priority Loan

Youths — College Fees

Education loans for young people who need financial support to pay college and university fees. Invest in a generation — loans disbursed directly to cover tuition and study-related costs.

Youth Loan

Property Owners

Property-backed loans for owners who need capital for renovation, development, or other investment purposes. Collateral assessment ensures fair loan-to-value ratios for exceptional amounts.

Secured Loan

Loan Interest Rate

30% per annum

All applicants are properly screened to verify repayment capacity. Guarantors play a key role, and collateral is required for exceptional / larger loan amounts.

The Process

How to Apply for a Loan

A straightforward, transparent process designed for accessibility.

1

Contact Us

Reach out to our Credit Officers by phone or WhatsApp to express your interest and discuss your needs.

2

Submit Application

Complete the application with the required documentation. A guarantor will be required to endorse your application.

3

Screening & Assessment

Our Credit Officers assess your capacity to repay. Larger loans include a collateral review to protect both parties.

4

Loan Disbursement

Once approved, your loan is disbursed promptly — designed for speed, especially in emergency financial situations.
